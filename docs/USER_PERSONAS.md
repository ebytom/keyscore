## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the user personas for AI Job Search Copilot. Personas are fictional, research-based representations of our target users that help the team make user-centered design and development decisions.

### 1.2 Research Foundation

These personas are based on:
- 50+ user interviews with job seekers
- Market research on job search behaviors
- Analysis of competitor user bases
- Industry reports on hiring trends
- Behavioral data from beta testing

### 1.3 Persona Categories

| Category | Definition | Count |
|----------|------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Core target users who will drive majority of engagement | 4 |
| **Secondary** | Important segments with specific needs | 4 |
| **Anti-Personas** | Users we explicitly do NOT design for | 2 |

#### Alex's User Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Discovering the Platform**
> Alex finds AI Job Search Copilot through a Reddit thread about career transitions. They sign up with Google OAuth, upload their manufacturing resume, and immediately run a skill gap analysis against a PM job at Stripe. The platform identifies 3 critical gaps and 5 "reframing opportunities" where Alex's experience maps to PM skills with better wording.

**Scenario 2: Weekly Usage**
> Every Sunday evening, Alex spends 2 hours on the platform. They analyze 3-4 PM job postings, generate tailored resumes for the top matches, and practice one mock case study interview. They track their applications and review AI feedback on their interview answers. Progress is visible in the dashboard.

**Scenario 3: Interview Preparation**
> Alex gets an interview at a Series B startup. They use Company Research to understand the product, interview process, and typical questions. They do 3 mock PM interviews focusing on product sense and execution. The AI feedback helps them structure STAR responses using their manufacturing experience.

## 4. Anti-Personas

These are users we explicitly exclude from our design decisions. Including them would dilute the product for our primary personas.

#### Anti-Persona 1: The Passive Browser

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   ANTI-PERSONA: THE PASSIVE BROWSER                         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                             │
│   CHARACTERISTICS                                                           │
│   • Employed and not actively seeking new role                              │
│   • Browses job postings "just to see what's out there"                     │
│   • No intention to apply or prepare                                        │
│   • Wants to compare salary without commitment                              │
│   • Will never convert to paid user                                         │
│                                                                             │
│   WHY NOT                                                                   │
│   Our platform requires active engagement—uploading resumes, analyzing      │
│   jobs, tracking applications. Passive browsers won't use core features     │
│   and would inflate our MAU without contributing to meaningful metrics.     │
│   They would request features like "anonymous salary lookup" that don't     │
│   align with our core value proposition.                                    │
│                                                                             │

#### Anti-Persona 2: The Automation Abuser

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

### 7.2 MVP Feature Set Justification

Based on persona analysis, MVP must include:

1. **Authentication** - All personas need accounts
2. **Resume Management** - Foundation for all AI features
3. **ATS Scoring** - Addresses pain point for Jordan & Sam (highest volume users)
4. **Chrome Extension** - Critical efficiency gain for Sam; differentiator
5. **Job Tracker** - Organization tool for high-volume users
6. **Cover Letter Generation** - Time-saver for all personas
7. **Basic Dashboard** - Central hub for activity

Phase 2 features (post-MVP):
- Mock Interviews (Alex, Morgan will pay premium for this)
- Skill Gap Analysis (Alex's primary need)
- Company Research (Morgan's primary need)
